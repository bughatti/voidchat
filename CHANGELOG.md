# VoidChat Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-17

- **Edit your tabs like Blizzard's:** right-click a tab to rename it, choose what it shows (message groups and each channel by name), move it, hide it, or make a new tab. The settings gear lists every tab to bring a hidden one back.
- **Settings per character.**
- The settings button is a real gear (it showed as a box).
- **First version.** A new chat window in place of Blizzard's: tabs (All, Whisper, Guild, Group, Trade, Loot, Void), a tab for each person you whisper, search, and copy (click a line, or C for the whole tab).
- **Who looted what.** The Loot tab lists every item, coin and currency line the game sends: yours and your group's, even when your chat settings hide loot. Search "staff" and you see who got it.
- **Void addon lines in their own tab**, out of your chat.
- Typing, slash commands and macros stay Blizzard's own. Chat history is kept for the session only: nothing anyone says is saved to disk.
