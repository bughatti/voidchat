# VoidChat - Chat Window, Copy Chat, Loot Log

**Works on:** Retail (Midnight) · WoW Forever

**A cleaner chat window that answers "who looted that?", lets you copy any line, and keeps addon spam in its own tab.**

## What it does

- **Tabs:** All, Whisper, Guild, Group, Trade, Loot and Void. A tab lights up when something new comes in.
- **Make the tabs yours, like Blizzard's.** Right-click a tab to rename it, pick what it shows (say, guild, party, loot, system, NPC talk, each channel by name...), move it, or hide it. Make your own tabs too: name one after a channel (LookingForGroup) and it shows that channel. Settings (the gear) brings hidden tabs back.
- **A tab for each person you whisper.** Right-click it to close it.
- **Who looted what.** The Loot tab shows every item, coin and currency line: yours and your group's, even when your chat settings hide loot. Search "staff" and you see who got it.
- **Copy anything.** Click a line to copy it, or the C button to copy the whole tab. WoW can't copy text out of chat; VoidChat can.
- **Search** the open tab.
- **Void addon lines in their own tab**, so your chat stays chat.
- **Short channel names:** [T] instead of [2. Trade - City].
- **Drag it, resize it:** it stays where you put it. It fades when you're not using it.

## Good to know

- Typing, slash commands and macros are Blizzard's own: VoidChat only replaces the window you read.
- Chat history lasts for the session. Nothing anyone says is saved to disk.
- In Midnight, the game hides chat text from addons in instanced combat. Those lines still show in VoidChat, but they can't be searched or copied.
- The combat log isn't shown.
- Your tabs and settings are kept per character.
- Want Blizzard's chat window back? Settings (the gear, top right) > untick "Hide Blizzard's chat window".
